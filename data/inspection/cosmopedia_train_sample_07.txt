Understanding Defrosting
------------------------

Defrosting is the process of thawing frozen food to bring it back to its original state before cooking. Microwave Defrosting: An Efficient Solution
------------------------------------------

While traditional methods such as leaving food in the fridge overnight or placing it under running water work well, these techniques require time and planning. Preparation Steps Before Microwave Defrosting

* Abnormal odors coming from your pet
* Non-healing wounds
* Persistent weight loss
* Change in appetite
* Difficulty breathing, urinating, or defecating
* Lethargy or depression
